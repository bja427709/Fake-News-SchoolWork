On ***May 14th of 2023*** at ***Cleveland Beach*** on ***8880 St Margarets Bay Rd*** ***Abby Zwicker*** went to sit down on a calm parkbench not to far from the water. As she went to sit down a Goose came out of what felt like Nowhere and spooked her causing her to scream. Soon after someone at the park nearby heard what happend, looked over and called animal control. When animal control showed up they moved in to try and remove the goose, but that caused the animal control member to get bit. After the goose bit the animal control member they decided the best choice was to leave the goose alone and let it go on its way.

After animal control backed away the goose stayed on the parkbench, and hissed at anyone who got to close. People started walking a longer way around it, while others stop'ed to watch from a safe distance. By the end of the afternoon people nearby were joking that the bench now belonged to the Goose. It looked like it had claimed its own waterfront property without paying a single dollar.

> Interview 1 — [***Abby Zwicker***], "I was just walking by the park, and went to go take a seat after my legs started to get sore. As I sat down this crazy goose jumped up next to me and started hissing. If I didnt run I probably would've been killed by that THING."

after hearing what happend some people didnt want to use the benches nearby incase another Goose showed up. Others came down just to see the goose that had scared off animal control. ***Abby*** said "she wouldnt be sitting there again anytime soon and would rather stand even in her legs got sore." The goose didnt seem bothered by all the attention and stayed on its parkbench like nothing had happend.

> Interview 2 — [***Alicia Rafuse***], "I was just at the park running around, I look over as a hear a scream and a see a girl (***Abby***) running from a park bench with a Goose on it."

> Interview 3 — [***Maci Rimersma***], "I got a call from a girl named ***Alicia*** saying something about a goose protecting a park bench. I was so confused until I got there and saw it myself. I went in with my cage attempting to remove the goose for the safty of everyone, but soon after I got attacked by it and we had to let it be.
By the next morning the Goose was gone and people could finally sit on the parkbench again. Nobody knew where it went but I dont think ***Abby*** will be taking any chances. She checked behind the bench before sitting down and kept an eye on the water the whole time."
